MONADE · STARS

119 626 stars read · source: hyg_v42.csv (HYG Database, astronexus.com, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Each scale is one Monade of 2 MB: a cube of 256 x 256 x 256 cells around the Sun, one bit per cell. A star lights the bit of its cell; the octal address of the cell (8 base-8 digits) is its place in the cube. The points drawn here are read back from the Monade itself, not from the catalog. The colours and the naked-eye filter are further Monadi with the same addresses: layers of the same map.

Mean error: average distance between a star and the centre of its cell, the price of one bit per cell. Sharing a cell: stars that fell where another star already was (one bit cannot count them; counters will, in step 5). Octants: the 8 parts of the cube, the first digit of every address.
