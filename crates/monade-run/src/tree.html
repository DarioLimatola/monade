The tree of all the Monadi

Every Monade has an octal address in one tree: each digit picks one of 8 parts (bit 0 → x, bit 1 → y, bit 2 → z). A word of 64 bits is 2 digits deep, a block of 512 bits 3, a Monade of 2 MB 8. The sky is a tree of 21 digits, from the cube of 16 777 pc down to cells of 0.008 pc, and only the branches that hold stars exist. Click one of the 8 parts to go down; the buttons go back up.

Brightness of a part: how many stars it holds (logarithmic). The small squares are the 64 parts of the next level.
