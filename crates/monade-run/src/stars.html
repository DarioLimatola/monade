Every star is one address of 21 octal digits (63 bits) in a cube of 16 777 pc around the Sun, down to cells of 0.008 pc: the place is exact, and only the stars are stored, not the empty space. Any region is a prefix of the address, so the mind can turn it into a dense Monade when it needs to compare regions. Colour from the colour index B-V; size and light from the magnitude.

Hertzsprung–Russell diagram of the stars within the radius: colour (left blue, right red) against true brightness (up bright). The diagonal band is the main sequence, where stars spend most of their life; the giants are above it on the right, the white dwarfs below on the left. Finding this relation from the data will be the first learning test on the stars.
